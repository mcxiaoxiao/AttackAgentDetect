\subsection{Task-held-out pair-association results}
\label{sec:pairwise-association-results}

The command-plus-artifact score lowers both error-rate point estimates relative to command TF--IDF and improves task-macro balanced accuracy and Hit@1. The same score was selected in all three outer folds. However, its absolute false-merge rate remains high, so the result does not support operational attribution.

The selected policy accepted all 148 observed same-task pairs and had no missed-link event in the 40 positive task groups. With no observed events, the ordinary bootstrap interval for the false-split rate is degenerate at zero. Under an independent-task-cluster sensitivity assumption, zero events in 40 groups gives a one-sided exact 95\% upper bound of 7.2\% on the proportion of groups with any miss; this is not a confidence interval for the task-macro false-split rate. The false-merge rate remains 75.87\%, meaning most different-task pairs are still linked. New task groups with Human and Agent traces recorded through comparable telemetry are needed for confirmation.
