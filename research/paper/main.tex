\begin{abstract}
Associating security traces across execution modes is difficult when traces share routine commands but differ in length and detail. We evaluate a pair score that combines command TF--IDF similarity with IDF-weighted containment of distinctive artifact names. In a task-held-out evaluation of 97 Human and Agent traces from 40 public CTF task groups, the command-and-artifact score improves task-macro balanced accuracy from 57.35\% to 62.06\% and Hit@1 from 29.60\% to 79.31\% relative to command TF--IDF alone. Its false-merge rate remains 75.87\%, so the results support exploratory retrieval analysis, not identity or attack attribution. The labels identify a shared CTF task only, and the trace sources use different collection interfaces.
\end{abstract}
